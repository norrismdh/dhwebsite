Where Digital Hive leads

Three areas where buyers consistently pick us

  • We never touch your data

    Digital Hive indexes metadata and passes users through to the live report - your data is never copied or moved into the platform. ZenOptics still harvests and stores rendered thumbnail snapshots and metadata. Staying metadata-only keeps data sovereignty intact and the security questionnaire short: native auth passthrough, no data movement

  • AI-powered search and analytics, today

    AI search runs across every connected tool, and AI-powered analytics is built in - not a context layer that exists to feed someone else's copilot. It ships now, and the deeper roadmap is shared in full under NDA

  • Adoption without the governance tax

    Certified, trusted content surfaces by default. There's no standing team of stewards manually certifying, deprecating, and re-curating every asset before users are allowed to trust it - the experience does that work for you

Where ZenOptics may fit better

Situations where their approach is the closer match

  • Governed AI workflow automation

    If agentic, governed execution - Maestro and ZIVA turning insights into automated, auditable workflows - is a primary buying criterion today, ZenOptics ships more breadth there than our search-and-analytics AI

  • Heavy KPI standardization across tools

    Teams whose top problem is reconciling conflicting metric definitions across a wide BI estate, with cross-tool glossary curation as the core workflow, tend to find ZenOptics' steward-driven, governance-first framing closer to their pain
